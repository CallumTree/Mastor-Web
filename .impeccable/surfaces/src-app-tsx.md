## Direction contract
THESIS: Every status is a site sign. Mastor speaks the UK safety-sign language everyone on site already reads: yellow needs doing, red is overdue or missing, green is done, blue is the one thing to do next. Refuses the category default of grey cards with a brand-coloured button.
OWN-WORLD: Near-black shell bars (#121316) over a cool concrete ground (#ECEEEF) with white work panels; flat sign plates in ISO safety colours (yellow #FFCD00 with black text, red #C1121F, green #0B7A3E, blue #0A55B5 with white text), square-ish corners (3px), no shadows, no texture. Barlow / Barlow Condensed (highway-sign grotesk) for everything; JetBrains Mono only for codes and refs; Cinzel only in the wordmark.
STORY: The visitor sees what is wrong, what is owed and what to do next before reading a sentence, acts on the blue plate, and trusts figures that say when they are incomplete.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Job home on a phone: black job bar (back, job name 24px Barlow 700 white, ref in mono grey, setup); three big figures on white in Barlow Condensed 700 with "+ N unpriced" where true; then "Action needed" as a stack of full-width sign plates (red first), each ending in a chevron; bottom nav black with the yellow capture key. Primary actions are blue plates.
FORM: Site safety signage, candidate 1 of 7 (user chose the pick over the rolled docket); seed key 5e5469aa. Signature move: one blue next-step plate ("➜ Price it", "➜ Chase the client") on every record.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
